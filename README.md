## Included Files

1. **`index.html` (Interactive Web Invitation)**:
   - **Sacred Om (ॐ) Motif** with Sanskrit Shloka (`॥ ॐ प्रजापतये नमः ॥`)
   - **Royal Typography** with gold shimmer effects for Soumyadip & Srabani
   - **Live Countdown Timer** counting down to **Sunday, 31st January, 2027 at 6:30 PM**
   - **Venue & Location**: Saha Complex, Dhamua with Google Maps button
   - **Sacred Wedding Ceremony Date**: Highlighted right below the venue (**Friday, 29th January 2027**)
   - **Add to Google Calendar** integration with pre-filled timings
   - **Authentic Bengali Shehnai Audio Player** (Ustad Bismillah Khan, looping seamlessly)
   - **Falling Golden Petals & Sparkle Canvas Animation**
   - **Guest Blessings & Wishes Board** with local storage support

2. **`card.html` (WhatsApp / Social Media Digital Card)**:
   - Formatted in 9:16 portrait ratio, ideal for WhatsApp chats, statuses, and stories.
   - Includes the Om motif, `॥ ॐ प्रजापतये नमः ॥`, Reception date (31/01/2027), and Wedding Ceremony date (29/01/2027).
   - Built-in **"Download Image (PNG)"** button that renders an ultra-crisp **1080×1920 high-resolution graphic** directly in your browser with no quality loss.

3. **`style.css`**:
   - Regal color palettes, metallic gold gradients, ornate SVG filigree corners, and responsive layout for mobile and desktop screens.

4. **`script.js`**:
   - Countdown timer logic, Bengali Shehnai audio player, falling flower petal engine, and guest message board.

## How to View and Share

- Simply double-click `index.html` or `card.html` to open them in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
- To share on WhatsApp:
  1. Open `card.html` in your browser.
  2. Click **"Download Image (PNG)"**.
  3. Send the downloaded high-resolution image directly to your friends and family on WhatsApp!
- You can also host `index.html` on GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify for free if you wish to give guests an interactive link.
